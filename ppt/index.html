SVC 主系表 · SVO 主谓宾

最重要的两种骨架

be / look / feel …　及物动词
主系表 SVC「是 / 变得 / 显得」
The sky looks blue.系动词常见：be, look, feel, taste, seem, become

There Be · 说『有』的句型

某处「有」东西，就用 there be

there is / there are
基本结构is 单数 · are 复数
There is a library on our campus.单数或不可数名词 → is There are two canteens near the dorm.复数名词 → are
常见错误 · Common Mistake
中文的「有」直译成了 have：✘ There have many students in the club. → ✔ There are many students in the club.
练There ___ some water in my bottle.（is / are）✔ is — water 不可数，没有复数，一律用 is。
就近原则看最近的名词
There is a pen and two books on the desk.be 与离它最近的名词一致 There are two books and a pen on the desk.换个顺序，动词跟着换
常见错误 · Common Mistake
✘ There are a lamp and some books on the desk. → ✔ There is a lamp and some books on the desk.（看最近的 lamp，别数总数）
练There ___ a lamp and some books on the desk.（is / are）✔ is — 就近原则：最近的名词是 a lamp（单数），所以用 is。

Negative & Question · 否定与疑问

把 be 拎到句首

isn’t / aren’t · Is there … ?
否定句isn’t / aren’t
There isn’t any milk in the fridge.否定句里 some 要改成 any There aren’t any free seats in the study room.复数用 aren’t

练___ there any clubs for freshmen?（Is / Are）✔ Are — clubs 是复数，疑问句同样要主谓一致。

Tenses · there be 也会换时态

变的只是 be，句型不动

was / were · will be · has been

练一练：找出正确说法

spot the right one
选出正确的说法 · Pick the right one
1.He gave me many useful ______ .✔ suggestions — suggestion 可数；advice 不可数。
2.______ forty students in our class.✔ There are — 「有」用 there be，不用 there have。
3.He is a student, ______ he works very hard.✔ and — 并列关系；一句一个连词，不与 although/because 连用。
4.The weather in Beijing is colder than ______ in Guangzhou.✔ that — that 代替 the weather，避免重复。
避坑口诀 · Shortcut
四大老坑自查：① 不可数名词不加 s；② 单数可数名词不裸用；③ there be 不是 there have；④ very 不修饰动词、从属连词不配对。

完形与填空：上下文定答案

context first
先通读再下笔read through
He was tired, so/because … 看前后句完形考逻辑：前后是因果、转折还是并列
常见错误 · Common Mistake
别只看空格所在句：答案常常藏在前一句或后一句。
练He was tired, ___ he slept.（so / because）✔ so — 前因后果（呼应 M17）。
语法填空看功能what does the blank do

练It is no use ___ about it.（to complain / complaining）✔ complaining — It is no use doing 固定句型。
